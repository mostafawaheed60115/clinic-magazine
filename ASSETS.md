# Asset provenance

Original logo and current five-color green/linen palette: supplied by the user in `assets/` and preserved unchanged.

Refined SVG symbol and logo: authored for this project, preserving the original face and heartbeat idea. The website now uses the supplied transparent raster logo directly; the authored SVG files remain available for future export needs.

`public/assets/clinic-logo-transparent.png` is the supplied transparent logo used by the interface and favicon.

Fonts: Cairo Variable and Manrope Variable, self-hosted through their Fontsource npm packages; their licenses are included in the installed packages.

Built-in image generation produced three fictional sample assets, converted to WebP with FFmpeg for delivery. No existing product branding was requested or depicted.

## Campaign prompt

Create a single photorealistic premium cosmetics still-life for a pink Egyptian B2B cosmetics magazine website. Wide landscape 3:2 composition. Pale blush pink seamless studio background, gently lit. A sophisticated arrangement of 5 unbranded skincare packaging objects: tall translucent dusty rose pump bottle, small frosted serum dropper bottle with silver collar, soft ivory moisturizer jar, pale pink tube, pale mauve cosmetic bottle, on low sculptural blush plinths. Dramatic but soft sunlight from upper left with real elegant shadows. A delicate pale fabric curl on lower left. The products occupy central 70%, clear margins around all objects, full bottles visible, beautifully detailed commercial beauty photography, tactile matte and glass materials. Palette pink #fac3f6, pale #fcf0fc, muted rose. No people, no text, no lettering, no logos, no watermarks. This is sample campaign artwork, not any existing brand.

Saved as `public/assets/campaign.webp`.
